## 2. Data and content

**What BDK has:**
- `decks.json`: 26 pages, 300 drug rows (generic, brand, category, classification, indication, activity, essential, label), and 1,840 cards of the form `{f, q, a, k, d:[drug indices]}`.
- Cards by source: Cards 1,236, EssentialKnowledge 373, SelectALL 187, Organisms 44.
- Cards by kind: category 300, indication 300, brand 289, generic 289, ek-kse 205, sa-indication 111, ek-notes 89, ek-bw 79, sa-category 60, classification 58, organism 27, sa-organism 17, sa-classification 16.
- A card has no options, no `why`, no `teach`, no `concept` and no `cite`.

**Could they become core questions?** Yes, by a generator (measured in the trial).
- `q` becomes the stem verbatim. The core's stem rule flagged 0 of 3,510, and the core does not require "Select all that apply." at the end.
- `a`, or `items` for the 883 list answers, becomes the correct option or options.
- Wrong choices must be generated and frozen.
- Each `why` is written from the table (the drug that owns the wrong choice, and its page); `teach` comes from the drug row; `cite` names the page.
- `concept`, `skill` and topic/sub are mapped by course design choices.

**What does not fit, measured:**
- 8 select-all cards have 10 to 16 correct items. They cannot meet the core's limit of at most 10 options with at least one distractor (test.js:85).
- 102 cards draw more than 10 options in BDK today. Fewer distractors would fix those.
- 215 "pick the item" stems are shared by several cards with different answers, so an exam paper could show two identical stems.

**What would be lost compared with how BDK asks:**
- Wrong choices that change at each showing, and mixed-up drugs offered first as wrong choices.
- Typed and self-check recall (1,641 cards).
- The "N correct" hint in Test mode.
- The drug rows as data that Compare and List read. In the core these would become reading-tab HTML or feature data.

**BDK's card ids are its question text** (`page|question`, plus the item for generated cards; mean 84 characters, max 518, 3 contain an apostrophe).
- Keeping them verbatim carries progress and the history's card codes over unchanged.
- But it roughly doubles saved storage (question 3), and BDK's rule 1 means any wording fix Louis decides changes an id.

## 3. Saved progress

**Measured:**
- After 6 answers BDK holds `bdk2:progress` (fields cards, days, confusions, match, `v:4`), `bdk2:settings` and `bdk2:log`.
- The core page opened with those keys present wrote nothing from them: no `bdk2:data:*`, no `bdk2:log:*`. It left them in place.
- The core reads only `<ns>:data:<profile>` and `<ns>:log:<profile>` (shell.html:619, 709).
- With `ns:'bdk2'` there is no key collision.

| Stored item | Core counterpart | Outcome |

| sessionStorage `bdk2:spot` | `<ns>:place` | Lost at the switch reload only |
| Exported file `{app:'bdk-quiz', exported, progress, log}` | Import needs `j.db.concepts` (views.js:2147) | Refused today. The converter must also run on Import |

**FSRS-4.5 into FSRS-7:** a converter is needed and it cannot be exact.
- A ts-fsrs 6.0.0 card has 12 fields, including a second stability `stabilityFast`. Default FSRS-7 has 34 weights, against BDK's 17.
- Recall at t = S measured between 0.9066 and 0.9171 for the values I tried. In FSRS-4.5 it is 0.9 by definition. So S and D have no one-to-one translation.
- The core rebuilds cards only by replaying a complete history. If the first answer kept is not answer 1, every answered question starts as new under FSRS and `DB.fsHistory='incomplete'` (shell.html:970-990).
- BDK keeps per-answer times only since v18 (c2f87ae, 2026-11-01). Earlier answers exist only as counts and the last 10 results per card.

**Result:** with today's core, every card Louis answered before 11/01 would start as new under FSRS-7. That changes its due dates and predicted recall right before the remaining quizzes. The alternatives:
- Replay per question, for cards whose whole history is in the log (a core change).
- Seed cards from S and D with a chosen `stabilityFast`. That value has no source, so it is Louis's decision.

**Behaviour changes even with a perfect converter:**
- Every answer updates memory, not only the first of the day.
- A miss returns after the library's 10-minute relearning step (measured default config), not "today".
- First right answer: FSRS-4.5 gives 4 days; FSRS-7 gives 4.78 days (measured).
